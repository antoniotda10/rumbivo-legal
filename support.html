Support & Account Deletion

For technical help, privacy questions, moderation, accessibility, or account deletion, contact antoniotda10@gmail.com.

Delete your Rumbivo account

  1. Open Rumbivo and go to Profile.
  2. Open privacy/account controls and select Delete account.
  3. Review the warning and confirm the request.

If in-app deletion is unavailable, email antoniotda10@gmail.com from the address associated with the account using the subject “Rumbivo account deletion.” Include only the information necessary to identify the account. We may verify ownership before deletion.

What deletion covers

Deletion removes or de-identifies the account profile, profile photo, preferences, and associated personal data. Some records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, financial reporting, or law. Backup copies are removed on the normal backup-expiration schedule.

You may also request deletion of selected profile media, submitted videos, support messages, or other personal data without deleting the entire account. Use the same support email, identify the specific data, and we may verify account ownership before completing the request.

Report safety or content

Use in-app report and block controls when available. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services; Rumbivo is not an emergency service. For urgent platform safety issues, email the support address with the subject “Rumbivo safety report.”

Response and accessibility

We aim to acknowledge support and deletion requests promptly. Accessibility feedback is welcome at the same address. You may write in English or Spanish.

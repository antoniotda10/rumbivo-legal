Privacy Policy

Rumbivo uses the minimum information needed to help people decide where to go out, protect the community, and improve the product. We do not sell personal data or use cross-app advertising tracking.

Who we are and how to contact us

Rumbivo is a nightlife discovery and social-planning application initially focused on Miami, Florida. Privacy, support, moderation, and accessibility requests may be sent to antoniotda10@gmail.com.

Information we process

  • Optional profile: display name, profile photo, gender selection, visibility preferences, incognito mode, nightlife intent, experience, and reliability signals.
  • Account and authentication: identifiers and contact information needed to create, secure, or recover an account.
  • One-time location checks: precise location is requested only after a clear action such as “Verify presence.” We calculate proximity to a venue and retain the resulting proof or coarse venue association, not a continuous location history.
  • Ambient pulse: with permission, the microphone may be sampled briefly to estimate an energy level. Raw audio is not uploaded, stored, or made available to other users.
  • Photos and clips: media you choose to upload, related metadata, and moderation status. Media may remain private while moderation is pending.
  • Product usage and diagnostics: interactions, crash information, device/app version, performance, anti-fraud signals, and other technical events. We avoid collecting free-form message content for analytics.
  • Safety and support: reports, blocks, moderation actions, appeals, and messages you intentionally send to support.
  • Purchases: product and entitlement information when paid features become available. Payment credentials are handled by Google Play or the applicable app-store payment provider, not stored by Rumbivo.

How we use information

We use information to provide maps, venue pages, presence and crowd signals, social features, personalization, account security, moderation, customer support, fraud prevention, product analytics, legal compliance, and service communications. We do not use microphone recordings or precise location for advertising.

Permissions and your choices

Location, microphone, camera, and photo access are requested in context after a clear user action. You can still explore venues, compare options, view public photos, open directions, and share plans without granting optional permissions. Rumbivo does not request background location, contacts, SMS access, Bluetooth access, or unrestricted access to your photo library.

Sharing and service providers

We may share only the information necessary with hosting, security, moderation, analytics, mapping, and customer-support providers operating under our instructions. When you choose directions, Google Maps receives the venue address or coordinates. We may disclose information to comply with law, protect users, investigate abuse, or complete a corporate transaction with appropriate safeguards.

Retention and security

Presence proofs are designed to expire quickly, normally within 15 minutes. Other data is retained only as long as needed for the purposes above, to resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. We use access controls, encryption in transit, least-privilege practices, and monitoring, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your rights and account deletion

You may update profile visibility, remove profile media, export account data, or request deletion. In-app deletion is available from Profile when enabled. You may also follow the instructions on our Support & Account Deletion page or email us. Deletion removes or de-identifies the profile and associated personal data, except information we must retain for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or law.

Age limits

Rumbivo is not directed to children. Social, matching, gifting, and nightlife participation features are intended only for people age 21 or older. We remove accounts or content when we learn that an ineligible minor is using restricted features.

International processing and changes

Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where providers operate. We will post material updates here and revise the effective date. Continued use after an update is subject to applicable notice and consent requirements.
